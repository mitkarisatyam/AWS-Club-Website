WHO WE ARE

ABOUT AWS SBG

AWS Student Building Group (AWS SBG) at Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, Pune is an active, student-led technical collective dedicated to cloud computing, artificial intelligence, software engineering, and emerging cloud technologies.

Our Purpose

OUR MISSION

To democratize practical cloud education for students. We bridge the gap between textbook concepts and enterprise engineering through hands-on architecture labs, hackathons, open-source project creation, and peer mentorship.

Our Vision

BUILD BEYOND THE CLOUD

To cultivate an engineering hub where students don't just consume technology — they architect it. We strive to empower students to build scalable systems, deploy intelligent models, and lead the future of cloud computing.

COMMUNITY PILLARS

01

Hands-on Engineering

Direct console practice and code-first sessions covering EC2, S3, Lambda, Containers, and Infrastructure as Code.

02

Collaborative Projects

Cross-disciplinary student teams building functional web platforms, machine learning pipelines, and cloud microservices.

03

Career Acceleration

Guidance for AWS certifications (Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect), industry talks, and student leadership opportunities.

READY TO BUILD WITH US?

Whether you are taking your first steps in cloud computing or building advanced AI workloads, AWS SBG VIT Pune welcomes your curiosity and drive.
